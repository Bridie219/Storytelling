A Case Study of Romance: Pride and Prejudice - Elizabeth's Declaration

Ritual Death

00:54

Lady Catherine's determination to come between Elizabeth and Mr Darcy reinforces the barrier of class, acting as a kind of moment of ritual death. However, Elizabeth is aware of the class system, and knows that Mr Darcy has the right to choose to wed any woman he pleases. Lady Catherine's objections relate to her plans to marry her own daughter to Mr Darcy, who she believes is a better match for him than Elizabeth.

Cardan seeks his revenge, reminding Jude that the little power she's won can be taken away as easily as her life. Cardan's constant threats towards Jude also create the barrier between them, but they also add to Jude's attraction to Cardan. She can't resist proving him wrong. She is determined to best him. A Romance Trope: Forbidden Love

Romantasy
Ritual Death

02:02

After their long-awaited kiss, Angel suddenly transforms into his vampire face, shattering the romantic atmosphere and catching Buffy off guard. Her shocked expression reflects the contrast between the man she has grown attracted to and the vampire she has been trained to fight. The transformation forces Buffy to confront the reality that her feelings for Angel conflict with her role as a Slayer. The abrupt shift from intimacy to fear highlights how their romantic connection is complicated by the very identities that bring them into opposition.
