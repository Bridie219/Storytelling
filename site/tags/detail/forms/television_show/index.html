A Romance Trope: Forbidden Love

Forms
Television Show

01:20

Angel’s evasive response about his age and Buffy’s playful curiosity establish a flirtatious dynamic that gradually builds romantic tension between them. His mysterious demeanour contrasts with Buffy’s willingness to engage with him, allowing their mutual attraction to emerge through teasing rather than direct confession. This exchange foreshadows their first kiss, as the playful conversation develops into a more vulnerable admission of desire when Angel reveals that all he can think about is kissing Buffy. The progression from subtle flirtation to an explicit expression of attraction makes their kiss feel like the culmination of the romantic tension building between them.

Forms
Television Show

02:02

After their long-awaited kiss, Angel suddenly transforms into his vampire face, shattering the romantic atmosphere and catching Buffy off guard. Her shocked expression reflects the contrast between the man she has grown attracted to and the vampire she has been trained to fight. The transformation forces Buffy to confront the reality that her feelings for Angel conflict with her role as a Slayer. The abrupt shift from intimacy to fear highlights how their romantic connection is complicated by the very identities that bring them into opposition.

Intrusive Fantasy: A Case Study of Shadowhunters

Forms
Television Show

00:00

The notion of protecting the Human World from the Demon World is what informs the audience of the secondary world that exists on top of the existing world. Clary's discovery of the intrusive world is described as her learning the truth about the world, because her true "Sight" allows her to see the monsters that have been infiltrating the human world.

This kind of fantasy is known as intrusive fantasy - where a secondary world begins to intrude on the existing world, with the intent of infiltrating or destroying the existing world.

Forms
Television Show

00:08

In intrusive fantasy, the secondary and existing worlds are often involved in conflicts, with a group that belongs to both worlds acting as the sentries, preventing the secondary world from crushing the existing world.

In Shadowhunters, the Nephilim are the ones preventing the demons from crossing into the existing world. They are from the world of Angels and Demons, as they are part Angel, and they are also part human. Their humanity gives them a reason to protect the human world from the Demon threat.

For Clary, coming to terms with the mission she faces is incredibly difficult.

Liminal Fantasy: A Case Study on The Chilling Adventures of Sabrina

Forms
Television Show

00:00

Sabrina seems like a perfectly normal teenaged girl, going home after a date. It seems very much like she has a normal life, until the door is closed to the "real" world, acting as a barrier between the world outside, and the magical world Sabrina exists in at home.

That is what makes The Chilling Adventures of Sabrina a liminal fantasy text, because she exists on the cusp of magic, pretending to live a normal life, but also existing in a world full of magic.

Forms
Television Show

00:21

As soon as she is home alone, the influence of the outside world fades away, and Sabrina begins using magic with a natural ease that makes it clear magic has been a part of her life for a very long time. The choice between two worlds could be explained as intrusive fantasy, but in this case liminal fantasy is more fitting. Sabrina will either have to reject or accept the magic that exists as a part of her life.

Forms
Television Show

00:41

The choice between her friends and family makes it clear that it's an internal struggle. Either Sabrina will accept her place in the magical world, or she will choose to reject it. The magic's continuing existence depends entirely on Sabrina choosing it.

Forms
Television Show

00:50

The ritual ceremony acts as an indicator of magic, but Sabrina's choice to leave before the ritual is complete makes it clear that she intends to reject the magic - or at least, reject what magic means to her family. Sabrina has to decide what she wants her magic to be like, in order to choose magic.

Forms
Television Show

01:11

Sabrina is also half human, half witch, so she's being torn between the worlds. She feels a connection to both, and to choose just one means rejecting the other. She is isolated from both worlds, because of her connection to both. She belongs to neither world entirely, which makes her choice that much more difficult. She has to find a balance between the human part of herself, and the witch part.

Forms
Television Show

01:44

The trailer makes it clear that whilst magic exists in Sabrina's world, her relationship with it is complicated. She uses it, but she also doesn't want to be consumed by it. She does not want to follow the dark path, instead, she wants to make her own choices. Her desire to resist the path she's being led down makes this text a liminal fantasy, because she straddles the border of the magical and nonmagical.
